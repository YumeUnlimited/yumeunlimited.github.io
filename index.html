HOW IT PLAYS

From spatula to factory.

010203
STEP 01

Cook by hand

Grill burgers, fry potatoes and pour drinks. Serving customers, washing dishes — at first, it’s all on you.

STEP 02

Let the machines work

Reinvest your earnings in automatic grills, item grabbers, dishwashers and conveyor belts.

STEP 03

Build the factory

Route the flow with splitters and scale a one-person kitchen into a fully autonomous burger factory.

BLUEPRINT VIEW

Plan it from above

Switch to Blueprint View to rearrange your restaurant. Move and rotate equipment, redesign your layout and plan out the perfect automation line.

CUSTOMIZE

Make it yours

Floors, tables, chairs, booths, lights, conveyor belts, even the entrance — design the burger shop of your dreams.
